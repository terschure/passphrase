PASSPHRASE

                    

                        

                            What happens when our voices enter digital systems
                            and become data? Who governs what that data becomes,
                            how it is used, and who has the authority to control
                            it? Our voices can become training data, synthetic
                            identities, tools for scams, and evidence in
                            immigration cases, but once a voice is digital, who
                            has the power to reclaim it?
                        

                        

                            Passphrase challenges you to give away your voice
                            (if you dare), and you must beat the system to claim
                            it back. It asks who is privileged enough to do so,
                            whether access to ownership is equal, and what the
                            consequences are when it is not.
                        

                        

                            Legislation is essential, but it does not guarantee
                            agency. Passphrase explores the gap between having
                            rights over our data and understanding what our data
                            can become or do in systems of power.
